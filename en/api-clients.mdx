You do not need to code to use what the API offers: project partners have already built ready-made services.

<Warning>
  These services are made and supported by their authors, not by ER:LC Russia. Check them yourself before use.
</Warning>

## Services

* **YaMRP Taxi** (https://mrptaxi.site/) — a taxi service on the Moscow RolePlay server and its management.
* **Moscow Tools** (https://moscowtools.github.io/mtools/) — game server moderation utilities.
* **MRP Helper** (https://mrphelper.erlcrussia.xyz/) — a helper for Moscow RolePlay staff.

<Note>
  We do not help choose a service and do not support them. Contact the support of the service itself.
</Note>

Before connecting a service:

* read its privacy policy and terms of use;
* make sure it is maintained and updated.
