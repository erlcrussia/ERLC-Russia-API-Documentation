## Key permissions

| Permission | What it allows |
|---|---|
| `ads.read` | Listing and viewing campaigns, analytics, and the Discord order state. |
| `ads.write` | Uploading a banner, creating a campaign, buying impressions, toggling, creating an order, and confirming payment. The permission is granted explicitly only and is not included in `full`. |

Pass the key in `erlcrussia-api-key` or `Authorization: Bearer <API key>`. The key acts as its owner: campaigns and coins belong to the account that created the key.

| Response | When |
|---|---|
| `401` | The key is missing or invalid, or its owner is inactive or banned. |
| `403` | The key lacks the required permission, is bound to a team, or has no owner. |
| `503` | Authorization is temporarily unavailable. |
